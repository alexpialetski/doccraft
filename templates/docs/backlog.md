# Backlog

Full prioritised backlog lives here. The working queue ("what next")
lives in [`queue.md`](queue.md); detailed story specs live under
[`stories/`](stories/).

Each priority tier has two subsections: **Planned** (active rows) and
**Shipped (reference)** (completed rows preserved for traceability).
When a story ships, move its row from **Planned** to **Shipped
(reference)** with a `Status: done (YYYY-MM-DD)` cell. The
`doccraft-queue-audit` skill enforces this split and the matching
Story-files coverage invariant.
